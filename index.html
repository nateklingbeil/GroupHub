How to use this app

  1. Create (or find) your group on Wise Old Man. Go to wiseoldman.net → Groups → Create group, and add every Ironman's exact in-game name. Already have a group? Open it and copy the number at the end of the URL (wiseoldman.net/groups/12345) — that's your Group ID.
  2. Install the RuneLite plugin. In RuneLite, open the Plugin Hub (wrench icon → Plugin Hub), search for "Wise Old Man", and click Install. This keeps everyone's XP and boss kills syncing to Wise Old Man automatically while you play — see the full steps below.
  3. Enter your Group ID above and click Load Group. The app pulls every member's stats straight from Wise Old Man — nothing to install, no login.
  4. Explore the tabs. Dashboard shows group-wide highlights, Members breaks down XP / XP gained / boss KC per player (click a row to see their full boss list), and Bosses shows the group's combined kill count for every boss, with the top hunter for each.
  5. Keep it fresh. Click ⟳ Refresh any time to re-pull the latest data, or change the "XP gained over" dropdown to compare day / week / month / year.
  6. Share it with your group. Once your group is loaded, click 🔗 Copy Link and send that link to your teammates (Discord, group chat, wherever). Anyone who opens it lands straight on your group's data — no typing a Group ID.

Setting up the RuneLite Wise Old Man plugin

  1. Open RuneLite and click the wrench icon in the top-right, then the Plugin Hub button at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Search for Wise Old Man and click Install.
  3. Open the plugin's settings (wrench icon → search "Wise Old Man" in your plugin list).
  4. Turn on "Update on login" (and optionally "Flash update button" or similar reminders) so your profile updates automatically every time you play.
  5. You can also click the plugin's sidebar panel icon in-game and press Update any time for an instant sync — handy right after a big boss trip.
  6. Make sure your RuneLite display name matches the name you added to the WOM group exactly (capitalization doesn't matter, spacing does).

Every member should install the plugin so the whole group's data stays current — this app only reads from Wise Old Man, it doesn't talk to RuneLite directly.
